Separating Axis Theorem (SAT)
Two convex shapes do not intersect iff there exists an axis onto which their 1-D projections (shadows) are disjoint. For OBB–OBB in 3D, exactly 15 candidate axes suffice: 3 face normals of A, 3 of B, and 9 edge cross-products Ai×Bj.
